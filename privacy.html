Privacy Policy

SkyGlide: City Skydive · Effective October 25, 2026

In short: the developer collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your progress (stars, best scores, coins, gear and settings) is saved only on your iPhone or iPad. Deleting the app deletes it.

Apple services the game uses

  • Apple Maps shows the 3D cities.
  • In-App Purchases are handled entirely by the App Store. The developer never sees your payment details.

Apple's handling of this data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Map data services

To make buildings and hills solid, the game downloads map tiles for each drop zone from OpenFreeMap (building outlines, © OpenStreetMap contributors) and the public Terrain Tiles dataset on Amazon Web Services (elevation). Like any website, these servers see your IP address and which map area was requested. Nothing that identifies you is sent.

Permissions

SkyGlide asks for no permissions: no location, camera, microphone, photos or contacts.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
