Every tower is solid.

Real building heights from OpenStreetMap make the city part of the game. Thread the gaps between Midtown's supertalls or play it safe and fly over them.

Paraglide

Open your wing. Read the air.

Speed bar, brakes and real gliding: every turn costs height. Glowing thermals lift you up to floating gold rings, and the wind pushes you off line.

Two modes · three cameras

Missions or Free Flight. Chase or pilot view.

Missions are ring courses scored to the last metre. Free Flight is pure soaring: hop out, open your wing and stay up as long as the thermals let you. Switch between chase, wide and first-person pilot view any time.

Questions

Is SkyGlide free?

Yes. New York, Paris and San Francisco are free, with every suit, wing and upgrade that isn't Sky Pass gear earnable by playing. The optional Sky Pass is a one-time purchase.

Does it need the internet?

Yes — the 3D cities stream from Apple Maps while you play.

Which devices?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 18 or later.

Does it collect my data?

No. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. See the privacy policy.
