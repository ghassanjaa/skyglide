How to play

  • Left stick — steer. Push forward to dive faster, pull back to slow down.
  • Rockets — hold for a boost. Every ring tops up the fuel.
  • Wingsuit — glide farther and faster in freefall.
  • Chute — open your paraglider before the PULL warning runs out.
  • Flare — hold just before touchdown for a soft landing.

The city doesn't appear

SkyGlide streams Apple's 3D cities, so it needs an internet connection.

Restoring the Sky Pass

Open Settings (gear icon) → Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with. It's also on the Sky Pass screen.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Progress

Stars, scores, coins and gear are saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.
